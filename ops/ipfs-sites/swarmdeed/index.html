What is a Deed?
A title deed for AI training data.
Every training pair scored by the Swarm & Bee tribunal receives a deed — a formal record with five independently verifiable proofs. Without a deed, it's a JSONL file. With a deed, it's a defendable asset.
Who validates the validator?
"Your scores are wrong." → Run the scoring function yourself. Deterministic.
"Your data was tampered." → Recompute the Merkle tree. Immutable.
"Your timestamp is fake." → Hedera aBFT consensus. Public. Permanent.
What makes a deed final?
Five layers of separation: PostgreSQL (query copy) → MinIO (versioned archive) → IPFS (distributed) → Hedera (consensus timestamp) → swarmdeed.eth (permanent URL). Each layer is independently verifiable. The deed survives even if Swarm & Bee disappears.
