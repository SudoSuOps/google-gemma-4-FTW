SECTION 02

Quality Methodology

Every pair passes through a dual-scale tribunal — two independent, unmodified base models scoring on separate hardware with zero shared bias. Each scale scores the pair twice (2-pass validation). If scores drift beyond 0.15 between passes, the pair is flagged for review and excluded from production delivery.
